As one of the co-organizers of the Bright & Bold Nights event, I am thrilled to share with you the incredible success of our second edition. It was an unforgettable night that brought together over 120 women for an evening of inspiration, connection, and celebration.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

With my co-organizer Laura Lesueur, we crafted a program that centered around three key themes: inspiration, connection, and celebration.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Firstly, we held an inspiring panel discussion on “Entrepreneurship as a Lifestyle” featuring incredible panelists Rokhaya Diallo, Louise Aubery, Ysaora Thibus, and Catherine Barba Chiaramonti. The discussion was engaging, thought-provoking, and left the audience feeling empowered and motivated to pursue their own entrepreneurial dreams.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

We also wanted to provide an opportunity for our attendees to connect with each other and share their own projects and ideas. The “Pitch & Troc” session was a hit, giving attendees the chance to pitch their projects and needs in just one minute. The energy in the room was electric as everyone cheered each other on and made new connections.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Finally, we celebrated the night away with plenty of opportunities to dance, chat, and enjoy delicious drinks. Our partners JOLIMOI and Mastercard helped to create an atmosphere of impact, audacity, sisterhood, and fun that was felt throughout the entire evening. Overall, Bright & Bold Nights was an incredible success and I can’t wait to see what the future holds for our community of inspiring women. <\/p>\n\n\n\n

We believe that it is important to create spaces where women can come together, connect, and lift each other up. We hope that our event has inspired and empowered everyone who attended, and we look forward to seeing what amazing things they will achieve in the future.
