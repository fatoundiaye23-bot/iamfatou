On Friday 9th December, I had the opportunity to participate in a panel discussion at the Cafet’Envi virtual event. The event featured various activities for entrepreneurs and business owners looking to start or grow their businesses. I was thrilled to be among the speakers invited to share my expertise on building confidence and audacity.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

The panel was hosted by Catherine Barba Chiaramonti, co-founder of Envi, L’ecole des independants, and featured Shanty Baehrel, founder of Shanty Biscuits, and myself, dubbed “the audacity queen” by Catherine herself.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

We shared insights and tips on how to boost confidence and build audacity, particularly for those considering starting a new venture or transitioning in their careers. We also had the opportunity to hear from other experts and entrepreneurs throughout the three-day event, including Eric Larcheveque, co-founder and CEO of Ledger, Marc Simoncini, founder of Meetic, and representatives from companies like Malt, L’Or\u00e9al, and Mazars.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

During the panel, we discussed the importance of mindset, authenticity, and taking risks to build confidence and audacity. We shared practical exercises and tools that participants could use to strengthen their confidence and build their audacity muscles.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

As an entrepreneur and business coach, it was an honor to share my experience and insights with the Cafet’Envie community. I hope that our discussion and the other activities offered at the event inspired and empowered participants to take the next steps in their entrepreneurial journeys.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

If you missed the event, be sure to check out Envi’s website for future opportunities to participate in their programs and events. And don’t forget to keep building your confidence and flexing your audacity muscle – it’s a lifelong journey!<\/p>\n\n\n\n
