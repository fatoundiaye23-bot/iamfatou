I had the honor of participating in a podcast series hosted by Roni Michael, Global Head of Innovation at KPMG International. The podcast, titled “I Travelled to the Future and Back,” invited me to imagine myself in the reality of 2040 and share what I have seen.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

In this episode, I discussed how many of the challenges around gender equality have been addressed in the years leading up to 2040. One of the major turning points was when women took to the streets and fought for their rights after a tragic event. This event sparked a change that ultimately led to gender equality becoming a non-issue in 2040.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

During the conversation, I highlighted the importance of having women in positions of power in business and politics. This shift in power dynamics has played a crucial role in reshaping the world in a more equitable way.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

I also expressed my hope that the overturn of Roe v. Wade could be the spark that ignites the changes I saw in 2040. It is important to remember that every small step towards gender equality is a step in the right direction.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

I am grateful to Michelle Carter for inviting me to join the podcast and to Roni Michael for facilitating such an insightful conversation. If you’re interested in hearing more about my vision of the future, you can listen to the podcast here<\/a>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
