And let me tell you, my trip to Mexico did not disappoint.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

One of the highlights of my journey was visiting Juchitan de Zaragoza, a small town in Oaxaca known for its rich cultural heritage. Here, I met with local artists, designers, and seamstresses who showed me their beautiful work and shared stories of their traditions. I was particularly drawn to the muxhe community, a group of individuals who identify as neither male nor female, and who hold a special place in the town\u2019s cultural fabric.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Another unforgettable moment came in Huatulco, where I had the privilege of meeting some of the last people in Mexico who still use snails to dye clothing. The process is long and intricate, involving hours of careful work to extract the vibrant colors from the snails\u2019 shells. It was incredible to witness firsthand the dedication and skill required to create such beautiful garments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

And finally, my journey led me to the bustling metropolis of Mexico City, where I had the opportunity to work with upcycling designer Alonso Murillo on a photoshoot. We created stunning looks using repurposed materials, showcasing the creativity and innovation that can come from sustainable fashion practices.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
