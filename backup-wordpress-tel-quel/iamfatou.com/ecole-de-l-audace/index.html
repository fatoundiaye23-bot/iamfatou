The curriculum

A real business, built in real conditions

Six key challenges give you the tools and methods to apply directly to your project, while you build the audacity and self-trust to actually use them.

01Personal development & HR tools
  • Align who you are with what you're building, for a brand story that sounds true, not a pitch you memorized.
  • Real methods to organize yourself, solo or with a team, when nobody's telling you what to do.
02Marketing & sales methodology
  • The Design Sprint and the Map Day method, to test an idea in days instead of months.
  • The Lean Canvas: a simple tool to define what you're offering, to whom, and why it's different from what already exists.
03Communication & branding
  • Build a visual identity, a pitch deck, and/or a website that actually look like your project.
  • Real ways to get noticed without a big-brand budget.
  • The basics of growth hacking: getting fast results with limited resources.
04Prototyping, user testing & community
  • Go from Lean to MVP, your product's first testable version, without spending six months on it.
  • Simple tools to prototype fast, even if you can't code or design.
  • Why a community gets built before the product, not after, and how to keep it engaged.
05Financial strategy
  • Market sizing: how to know if your market is big enough to make a living from.
  • Build a business model that holds up, using the Sustainable Business Model Canvas as your guide.
  • A first draft of your business plan. No unnecessary financial jargon.
06Pitching & growth
  • Build a pitch deck people actually want to keep listening to.
  • The art of the two-minute elevator pitch, without sounding like you're reciting it.
  • Plan for the talent you'll need around you, the day this actually takes off.

Assessment: one required written case study on launching a business in a real-world setting. It earns you a KEDGE Business School certificate, aligned with Skill Block 6 of the KEDGE Bachelor's program, “Leading an Operational Action Plan.”

The Fatou method

The 7 commandments of entrepreneurial audacity

The throughline of Fatou's opening masterclass for KEDGE Entrepreneurship. Train it all year long.

1

Dare to reveal yourself

Try this

The 50 facts exercise. Write down 50 things that make you unique. Experiences, values, wins.

2

Dare to move without mastering it all

Try this

Decide with 70% of the information. Don't wait for certainty.

3

Dare to keep learning

Try this

Block one non-negotiable hour a week to learn something outside your expertise.

4

Dare to experiment (and fail)

Try this

One experiment a month, followed by a learning review every time. What did I learn?

5

Dare to change your mind

Try this

Once a quarter, take a strong belief and ask yourself, what if the opposite were true?

6

Dare to speak up

Try this

Share one real opinion a week. Know how to tell your story in two minutes.

7

Dare to keep dreaming

Try this

Every six months, ask yourself, if I had zero fear, what would I try right now?
