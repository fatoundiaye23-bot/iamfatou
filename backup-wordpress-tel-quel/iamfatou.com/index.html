chiefaudacityofficer

Audacity isn't a nice-to-have. It's what drives innovation, keeps top talent, and makes culture perform.

I help leaders and organizations turn the status quo into bold action, and culture into performance.

Whether it's a keynote or a multi-year leadership program, my job is to change what happens after I leave. Not inspire for an afternoon. Shift how your people lead, decide, and show up when it counts. Audacity is the most underused leadership lever there is. In a world being rewritten by AI and constant disruption, the leaders who win won't be the most credentialed. They'll be the most courageous: willing to act before every variable is known, and to make bold calls when the old playbook stops working.

That's the premise behind The Audacity Method™. I'm not here for a one-off jolt of inspiration. I bring the spark, then build what makes it hold: a shift that becomes habit, individually and collectively, until it's culture.

I don't teach boldness in theory. At 6, I lost my home overnight. At 13, a year in a hospital bed taught me what freedom feels like. At 26, I learned my skin color could close doors. At 33, I closed my own company and rebuilt from zero. Audacity is the operating system I've had to run my whole life, and the one I now teach. That's why Chanel, Danone, Nike, LCL and Microsoft bring me in: not for a motivational hour, but for a shift their teams still feel months later.
