My platforms turn bold action into change people actually feel.

Building narratives that make audacity, culture and reinvention feel possible.

As an opinion leader, I use storytelling and creativity through film, audio, and the written word to reshape perceptions and build a more compassionate, equitable society, inspiring others to join a community of change-makers.

For three years, I traveled the world as author and presenter of this CANAL+ documentary series, rediscovering global craftsmanship, culture and sustainability through the lens of fashion. Season 1: Mexico, Mongolia, Greece, Scotland. Season 2: Senegal, Thailand, India, Argentina. Each episode is a story about the people behind the fabric: artisans, designers, and communities reinventing their craft, and about what it takes to build a more inclusive, culturally aware industry. It airs as part of Les Nouveaux Explorateurs.

The podcast that dares you to move. Dare to overcome fear. Dare to change. Dare to try. Dare to start. Dare to fail. In short, dare to take action. Audacity is a muscle: the more you use it, the stronger it gets. Each episode of my podcast, L'Audace cette Badass, I open by sharing a piece of my own life, then sit down with a guest who chose to flex their own courage. I close with a challenge, because courage doesn't grow on its own — it grows when we practice. Produced by Kikina Studio.

Diary of an Audacious Woman is where I put down the polished version and write the real one: the stories behind the speaker, the bold moves, the bad days, the becoming. I'm not writing as an expert, just someone who's lived. New entries on Substack.

I also hosted Bold Thinkers, a podcast dedicated to igniting the ideas shaping the future of business and society for the HEC Paris Impact Company Lab, traveling to global innovation hubs to amplify the voices of the people building what's next. Episodes recorded in Kinshasa, Mumbai, São Paulo and Cairo.

Because if I'm going to talk about audacity on stage, I have to live it on the page too.
